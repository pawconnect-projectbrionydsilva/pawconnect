A LITTLE KNOWLEDGE, A LOT OF LOVE

Care makes a difference.

Simple, thoughtful ways to make your neighbourhood safer for animals.

🥣
01 / FOOD & WATER

Offer food and fresh water

Place clean water in a safe, shaded spot. Offer suitable food and keep feeding areas tidy.

🩺
02 / HEALTH

Encourage timely treatment

Injured or unwell animals may need a qualified veterinarian. Avoid giving human medicines.

🏡
03 / ADOPTION

Choose responsible adoption

Think about time, space and long-term costs before adopting. A pet is a lifelong commitment.

🤍
04 / KINDNESS

Be gentle and patient

Approach unfamiliar animals calmly, give them space and never force contact.

🐾

Be kind. Be informed. Be their friend.

Small everyday actions help build a community where animals are treated with compassion.
